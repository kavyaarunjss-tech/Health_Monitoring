## Notes shown under the limits table

- India: India's 24-hour (and 8-hour) limits must be met 98% of the time in a year; they may be exceeded on 2% of days, but not on two consecutive days [9].
- WHO: WHO 24-hour guideline levels are set at the 99th percentile, i.e. about 3-4 days a year above the level are expected even where the guideline is met [8].
