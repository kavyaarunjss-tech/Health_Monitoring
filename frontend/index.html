    How to read the ranges and confidence

    The likely range is built from past forecast errors: 80% of earlier forecasts at that horizon were off by an amount inside the bar; about one in five actual values fell outside. Confidence follows the horizon: high up to +6 h, moderate for +12 to +24 h. The main pages show only these four horizons (+1, +6, +12, +24 h). Longer horizons are listed above as experimental: in our tests +36 h was mixed (better than persistence in the validation period, not in the test period) and +48/+72 h had no consistent skill over persistence, so they are shown for transparency only. Alerts: "Likely" pop-ups go up to +24 h, "Possible" only up to +12 h. The personal alert thresholds are a design decision derived from the cited sources, not official triggers.
